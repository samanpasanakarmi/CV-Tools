# Customer handover and release readiness

This is a proposed checklist, not evidence of customer approval or completed delivery.

- [ ] Confirm product scope and requirements with customer using [traceability matrix](REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md).
- [ ] Confirm repository, license/ownership and Supabase administration responsibility.
- [ ] Verify CI, production build and approved HTTPS demo URL.
- [ ] Complete [test plan](../testing/TEST_PLAN.md), [test results](../testing/TEST_RESULTS.md) and live security checks.
- [ ] Agree privacy notice, retention, account deletion, support and incident contact; complete required implementation.
- [ ] Verify all example files/screenshots use fictional data before sharing.
- [ ] Walk through current [user guide](../user/USER_GUIDE.md) with customer; record feedback and any unresolved issues.
- [ ] Confirm genuine team contributions and course-required Scrum evidence privately.
- [ ] Agree final release commit/tag, deployment instructions, customer-approved delivery format and next owner.
- [ ] Record actual sign-off date, approver and evidence reference in approved storage.

Do not publish credentials or confidential meeting records in this public repository. AI is outside core release; any future AI delivery needs separate approval and verification.
