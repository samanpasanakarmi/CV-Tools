## Promises
1. Attend and actively participate in Scrum **five days per week**; record each (max 15 min) and upload videos to the team's **Metropolia Google Drive** (not the public repository).
2. Each person says **yesterday / next / blockers**; discuss difficult problems after the meeting.
3. Keep Issue board updated. Tell others early about absence, delays or blockers.
4. Work on tasks using branches and PRs; request one teammate's review before merging.
5. Make frequent genuine commits; don't commit under another member's identity.
6. Decisions: discuss options, note the decision and owner, then support the team's choice.
7. No sharing of real CV personal information, credentials, or non-consented meeting recordings in the repo.
8. All members participate in customer presentations and document their own work honestly.
